Our Story

A Symphony of
Science & Touch

At Bethlehem Lebneh, located in the heart of Queen Anne, Seattle, we specialize in luxury skincare, wellness, and holistic beauty services to help you embrace a golden approach to life.

From rejuvenating treatments to personalized wellness plans, we empower you to enhance your skin, well-being, and overall lifestyle. Our expertise will guide you seamlessly through your personalized path to beautiful skin.

The Practitioner

Bethlehem Lebneh

With a profound understanding of holistic wellness, Bethlehem’s philosophy is deeply rooted in her background as a dedicated yoga instructor. Having practiced Kundalini yoga for 3 years, she believes that the skin is a direct reflection of inner vitality, and true radiance can only be achieved when mind, body, and spirit are in alignment.

From her perspective, the mindful intention cultivated in yoga translates beautifully into clinical skincare. Bethlehem approaches every facial contour and skin concern with a highly intuitive touch, seamlessly combining this serene foundation with an uncompromising expertise in advanced, results-driven esthetics.

Whether she is working with Bio-Therapeutic microcurrents, precise chemical exfoliation, or targeted hyperpigmentation regimens, Bethlehem’s goal is to bring harmony back to the skin. Instead of aggressively stripping the barrier, she focuses on understanding, deeply nourishing, and gently guiding the skin into its optimal, healthiest state of regeneration.
